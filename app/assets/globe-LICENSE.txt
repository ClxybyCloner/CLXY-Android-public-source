Natural Earth geography

The globe uses public-domain Natural Earth map data.
Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

All versions of Natural Earth raster and vector map data are in the public domain.
They may be used, modified, and distributed for personal, educational, and
commercial purposes without permission or attribution. Natural Earth provides
the data without a warranty of accuracy or fitness for any particular use.

The bundled outlines use the 1:110m country polygons. Country markers use the
1:10m country label coordinates, including small countries and territories.
Unused attributes were removed; the outline coordinates are not rescaled.
These country-level markers and IP-based locations are approximate, not GPS.
They do not establish a person's physical location.
